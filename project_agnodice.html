Advancing Women's Health Research

Project Agnodice is dedicated to illuminating the systemic discrepancies in medical research and healthcare funding. We aim to bridge the gap in understanding conditions that predominantly or differently affect women.

Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) & PMDD

Despite affecting a vast majority of menstruating individuals, PMS and its severe counterpart, PMDD, have historically been dismissed in clinical research. This section outlines the history of this dismissal and current clinical findings.

Key Findings:

  • Historical categorization as a psychosomatic condition.
  • Current funding disparities compared to non-sex-specific conditions.

Methodology

Project Agnodice relies on rigorous data collection and peer-reviewed analysis. Our approach includes:

  1. Comprehensive literature reviews of medical journals from 1990 to present.
  2. Analysis of public funding databases.
  3. Interviews with leading researchers and clinicians in women's health.
